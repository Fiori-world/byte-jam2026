How do I get started?

After clicking play you will be brought to this screen.

This is the credit screen where you will decide what you will take with you into your bunker, the amount of credits you have is based on the difficulty you choose.

  • Easy - 20 Credits
  • Normal - 15 Credits
  • Hard - 10 Credits

As seen in the screenshot above, you have a limited amount of credits to spend on resources to take out into the bunker. You can use your credits to stock up on Rations, or Waters, you can even use them for tools used to scavenge the wasteland, such as a Radio to warn you of dangers, a map to choose the locations you visit, first aid kits and medicine, a gas mask to increase your survival chance, and a crowbar to help you scavenge better.

Each of these items cost a varying amount of points so choose wisely how you spend them!

Basic Gameplay Loop

Now that you've chosen your supplies and headed into your bunker you have to strategize how to survive the longest possible time! you will need to ration your food and water as well as deal with any events that may arise.

Events can vary on if they will be good for you or bad for you, the harder your difficulty the more likely you are to get bad events.

You can proceed to the next day by using the hammock in the bunker to sleep, you can also head out through the hatch and scavenge for more supplies in the wasteland, be careful as there will be enemies and hazards that can injure you or make you sick. You'll have to use all your resources wisely to last as long as you can in the bunker!
